# Independent audit, round 2

I decoded only numeric `sheets[].cells` in global `order`, cut verses by raw `s/e`, and formed written units by `01_HOW_TO_READ.md`. I did not read predecoded grid letters, run reports, or project checkers for a result. The only run results consulted were W138 row identifiers so that the ten requested random rows could be selected. The published witnesses are saved under `sources/`, with exact byte URLs and SHA-256 in `SOURCES2.json`.

Identity is exact written-unit matches in a semiglobal alignment (+2 match, −1 substitution, −2 gap; free carrier flanks). D8 instead uses local +2/−1/−1 alignment between two raw columns. Each shuffle maximum comes from 1,000 seeded (20261026) permutations of the selected carrier units, with the same score recomputed. D7 shuffles heading order. The textual CONFIRMED gate is ≥75% over ≥40 units and above every shuffle. WEAK denotes recognizable but subthreshold wording; D10 is an arithmetic check, so identity and shuffles are inapplicable.

D1 carrier positions were found by scanning the 24 first/end positions and both directions and three-unit phases in ch.44–45; the table reports the best routes, with shuffles of those selected columns. D3 and D4 also scan several positions. These are descriptive controls, not a correction for the search over routes. The Jinasahasranāma web text numbers two different verses 19; the matching one is its introductory verse, while the later hymn verse 19 has at most 7/40 in this scan.

## Reference and rule provenance

The non-guide extraction rules used are the marked-group interval interpretation from `runs/w142/PREREG.md` and its named provenance files for D5, and the position/direction family in `runs/w144/PREREG.md` and `runs/w156/PREREG.md` for D1/D4. I inspected only the `group_map` extraction logic in `runs/w142/build.py` to identify the group provenance paths and interval convention; no W142 streams or results entered the scores. Ch.39 uses only rows whose provenance says `marks as read`; ch.42 uses `runs/step4_copyist/groups_ch42_v3m.json` inclusive zero-based intervals. D5 therefore audits the reproducible marked subset, not every proposed copyist row. The ten D9 row identifiers were selected with `random.Random(20261026).sample` after sorting W138 eligible rows by chapter, column, and reference and restricting to ordinary E1/E2 long, short, or merged columns. No W138 letters or scores entered the recomputation.

The exact source-byte checksums are in `SOURCES2.json`. `audit2.py`, `group2.py`, and `align2.cpp` contain the extraction, scoring, and controls. Rebuild with `rtk c++ -O3 -std=c++17 -shared -fPIC reports/independent_audit/align2.cpp -o reports/independent_audit/align2.so`, then run `rtk python3 reports/independent_audit/audit2.py` and `rtk python3 reports/independent_audit/make_report2.py`. The Ādipurāṇa comparison uses the independently downloaded 1956 volume’s OCR of 23.192, which has visible corrupt letters. The online Ācāryabhakti page is in Prakrit despite its Sanskrit title, so its diagnostic row cannot establish the Sanskrit claim.
