Help

We can read the letters. We need people who read the words.

All 20,640 verses are in Kannada letters, each with a first machine translation into English. Much of that English is wrong. The poem needs readers of Old Kannada.

1

Open a chapter

Every verse is shown in Kannada, in Roman letters and in rough English.

2

Suggest a correction

Under the verse, “Suggest a correction” opens a short form on GitHub with the verse already filled in. You need a free GitHub account.

3

Others review it

Your correction becomes a proposed change on GitHub. Other readers check it and approve it.

4

It goes into the text

Once two readers, including a maintainer, approve it, it goes into the site and the download automatically, with your name on the verse.
