Hidden texts

Take one letter from each verse of a chapter, such as the last, and read them in order. In many chapters those letters spell a verse from another book: the Bhagavad Gītā, or a Jain hymn.

The letters, in order, and the book they spell

Each passage here was rebuilt from the numbers and passed three tests. At least three in four letters match the book. It beats 1,000 shuffles of the same letters. It also beats 1,000 other verses of the same length.

    Tamil, Telugu and ten other languages

    We searched for running text in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Tulu, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Odia, Hindi, Pali, Apabhraṃśa and Ardhamāgadhī, using full libraries of each: about 1.9 billion sounds. The search covered every page, many routes, letter places from the start and end of each verse, and 14 different number-to-sound tables. Test passages planted in the grids were found. The real grids gave none.

    The other reading shapes

    Chapter 4 names dozens of shapes for reading a page. Only the wheel is known. We drew 47 shapes from their names, in every turn and mirror, and read every page with each. None gave new text.
