      How the check works

      Each number is a sound. We join the sounds into letters, then find the Kannada words of six sounds or more that fit, without overlapping. The score is the share of sounds that fall inside words.

      Then we shuffle the same letters 200 times and score each shuffle. Real text in reading order beats every shuffle. Letters in a random order do not. The poem's own route beats them all.

      Dictionary: the Alar Kannada dictionary by V. Krishna (ODbL) and Wiktionary Kannada (CC BY-SA), 114,365 words.
